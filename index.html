5:20 PM

Based on a true story

The Silhouette Behind My Monitor

Read it alone, with the lights off.

5:20 PM. It was around 5:20 PM on a regular working day. I was alone in the office, and the lights were off. The whole place was quiet, with only the faint hum of the computers breaking the silence.

I was sitting at my desk, doing my usual work, when I noticed something across my table.

Behind my monitor, I saw what looked like the silhouette of a person standing directly in front of me.

At first, I thought it was one of my coworkers.

So, without thinking too much about it, I casually called out.

“Uy!”

No response.

I looked at the silhouette again.

“Uy, unsa man ka diha?”

Still nothing.

I started getting a little annoyed, thinking maybe my coworker was just standing there, trying to get my attention or messing around with me.

So I called out again.

“Uy m? Nag unsa ka diha, bosseng?”

Nothing.

The figure remained behind my monitor.

That's when I decided to stand up and take a proper look.

But the moment I started rising from my chair, something happened that made my entire body freeze.

The silhouette went down.

It moved at exactly the same time I did.

As I stood up, it lowered itself behind my monitor, as if it was deliberately trying to hide from me.

I stopped moving.

For a few seconds, I just stared at the space behind my screen.

“Unsa man ni oy?”

I tried to convince myself that it was just a coworker crouching behind the table. Maybe someone was picking something up. Maybe there was a reasonable explanation.

But why wasn't the person answering me?

And why did it move the moment I stood up?

I slowly moved toward the end of the aisle and leaned over to see who was there.

I was expecting to find one of my coworkers hiding behind the table.

But when I looked—

WALA.

There was nobody there.

The entire aisle was empty.

I looked around, trying to find anyone who could have been standing behind my monitor just seconds ago.

Nobody.

No footsteps. No movement. No one walking away.

Nothing.

I swear, the silhouette had been right in front of me.

I had spoken to it.

And I had watched it move.

My heart started racing. I didn't even bother checking behind the other tables anymore.

I immediately rushed to the light switch and turned on all the lights.

The entire office lit up.

I looked back at my table, at the monitor, and at the aisle where I had seen the silhouette.

Everything looked normal.

But I wasn't staying there to find out anything else.

I rushed back to my desk, grabbed my things, and packed everything as quickly as I could.

I didn't care that my shift wasn't over yet.

I didn't care if someone questioned why I was leaving early.

Gusto na gyud ko mouli. Dili na ko ganahan magdugay didto.

I left the office as quickly as possible.

Even after I got home, I kept thinking about what had happened.

Maybe my eyes had played tricks on me because the office was dark.

Maybe there was some explanation I hadn't considered.

But there's one thing I still can't get out of my head.

When I called out, the silhouette didn't respond.

When I stood up, it went down.

And when I finally checked the aisle…

Walay tawo.

Hangtod karon, wala gihapon ko kahibalo kung unsa gyud to akong nakita niadtong adlawa.

Pero usa ra gyud akong nahibaw-an.

Pag-abot sa alas 5:20 sa hapon, abi nako ako ra ang nahibilin sa opisina.

Ambot lang kung ako ba gyud ang nag-inusara.

The End.
